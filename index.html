01 Introduction

Welcome to Viora TV IPTV Player (“Viora TV,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”). We respect your privacy. This policy describes how information may be handled when you use the application.

Viora TV is a media player designed to play compatible streams and playlists supplied or selected by the user.

02 Information We May Handle

The information involved depends on the app features you use and the services integrated into your version of the app.

  • Playlists and streaming sources: URLs, playlist data, channel names, or playback details you enter may be processed to provide playback.
  • Technical information: Device type, operating system, diagnostics, and crash information may be processed to maintain reliability and troubleshoot problems.
  • Advertising data: If ads are enabled, advertising partners may process device identifiers, ad impressions, and related technical data under their own policies.
  • Information you provide: If you contact support, we may use the information you choose to include in your message to respond.

03 How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide app features and compatible IPTV playback.
  • Load and manage playlists and streaming sources.
  • Diagnose errors, improve stability, and maintain security.
  • Display and measure advertisements, if advertising services are enabled.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

04 IPTV Playlists & Third-Party Content

Viora TV is a player and does not itself guarantee the availability, accuracy, or legality of content supplied by third-party sources. When you play a stream, the provider of that stream may receive technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver the content.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the rights and permissions required to access the content you choose to play.

05 Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or technical functionality. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Review the policies of any third-party services used by your version of the app for details about their data practices.

06 Data Storage & Security

Some preferences or playback-related information may be stored locally on your device to support app functionality. The precise data stored depends on the features you use.

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

07 Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate app features, deliver ads, troubleshoot technical issues, comply with law, or protect legal rights.

08 Children's Privacy

Viora TV is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

09 Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

You can stop using the app or remove locally stored app data through your device settings. Information handled by third-party services may need to be managed directly with those providers.

10 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. Any revised version should be published with an updated date at the top of this page.
